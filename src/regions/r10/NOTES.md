# Region 10 notes (for the core)

Nothing in the core has to change for this pack. Two small suggestions:

- **Bridges over open sky.** `bridge-h`, `bridge-v` and `stepping-stone`
  draw water under themselves, so they look wrong on `sky` ground. The
  Cloud Capital uses `carpet` over `sky` (`M` in its legend) for its
  lacquered walkways instead. A `cloud-bridge` tile, or bridges that draw
  their `under` ground, would let sky maps use real bridges.
- **Words from the Tower.** The Tower (region 5) comes after this region,
  so its words (飛ぶ, 心, 夢…) aren't used in this pack's sentences or drum
  calls. 塔 also appears only in kana (そうぞうの とう).
